What is SAP ARIBA?

SAP Ariba Sourcing & Procurement

SAP Ariba is a cloud-based procurement and supply chain management solution that helps organizations streamline sourcing, supplier collaboration, contract management, and purchasing operations. It enables businesses to improve efficiency, manage supplier relationships, and drive smarter procurement decisions across the entire source-to-pay lifecycle.

Goals

Equip learners with in-depth knowledge of SAP Ariba workflows, sourcing creation, supplier onboarding, and procurement process management.

Audience

Procurement professionals, sourcing analysts, consultants, project managers, and IT users seeking Ariba expertise.

Prerequisites

A basic understanding of procurement, business processes, or enterprise applications is recommended for this course.

Content

Hands-on modules cover sourcing, contract management, supplier lifecycle, purchase requests, and procurement analytics.

Course content

What will you learn in SAP Ariba Course?

SAP Ariba sourcing processes, supplier onboarding, and collaborative requisition cycles

Contract management, catalog management, and purchasing workflows for enterprise procurement

Supplier performance, compliance tracking, and analytics to improve sourcing decisions
