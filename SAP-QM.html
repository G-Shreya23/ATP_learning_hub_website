Quality management & process control

What is SAP QM in S/4HANA?

SAP QM (Quality Management) helps organizations plan, inspect, and monitor the quality of products, materials, and production processes across the supply chain. In SAP S/4HANA, QM enables businesses to manage inspections, quality notifications, and corrective actions in a way that supports operational excellence and compliance.

Goals

This program is designed to help you process crucial Quality Management activities in SAP S/4HANA along the logistics supply chain, define quality inspections and inspection planning, support material and production quality processes, identify improvement opportunities using QM functionalities, and strengthen business processes with data-driven quality controls.

Audience

This training is ideal for SAP consultants, quality managers, production and procurement teams, supply chain professionals, and business users who want to improve product quality, compliance, and process control in SAP S/4HANA.

Prerequisites

A basic understanding of SAP logistics, production, or warehouse processes is helpful, but the course is designed to build the essential QM concepts progressively for working professionals and functional learners.

Version

The curriculum is aligned to SAP S/4HANA 2021 and focuses on the Quality Management workflows and functional setups used across manufacturing, procurement, and logistics operations.

Course content

What will you learn in this SAP QM course?

Understand the SAP QM architecture and the role of Quality Management in S/4HANA operations.

Plan and configure quality inspections and inspection lots for incoming, in-process, and outgoing checks.

Manage material and batch-related quality procedures across procurement, production, and logistics processes.

Integrate SAP QM with MM, PP, SD, and warehouse processes for end-to-end quality control.

Monitor quality notifications, defects, and corrective action processes to reduce rework and risk.

Use quality management controls to strengthen process compliance and improve product and customer satisfaction.

FAQ

FAQs: SAP S/4HANA QM Course and Career

What is SAP QM and why is it important?

SAP QM helps businesses manage inspection plans, quality checks, and defect handling throughout the supply chain. It plays a key role in ensuring compliance, reducing defects, and improving product and process quality.

Who should do this course?

This course is ideal for SAP consultants, quality engineers, production planners, logistics professionals, procurement specialists, and business users who want to build expertise in quality management processes within SAP S/4HANA.

What core areas does SAP QM cover?

The course covers inspection planning, quality notifications, material quality checks, batch management, supplier quality control, product compliance, and integrated process monitoring across SAP modules.

How does SAP QM connect with other SAP modules?

SAP QM integrates closely with MM, PP, SD, and logistics processes, allowing organizations to manage quality requirements throughout the value chain with greater traceability and control.

How does this training prepare me for global certification and business roles?

The course builds practical knowledge of quality management in SAP S/4HANA, giving learners a strong foundation for implementation work, process support, and functional roles in enterprise quality operations.

What are the salary prospects for SAP QM professionals?

SAP QM specialists are valued across manufacturing, process industries, and supply chain transformation programs. Compensation depends on experience and geography, but the skill set remains highly relevant in quality and operations roles.

Can I take this training while working full time?

Yes. The program is designed for working professionals with flexible classroom or online learning options and practical business case examples that support professional upskilling.
